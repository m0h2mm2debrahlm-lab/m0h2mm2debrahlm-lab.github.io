Profile

A graphic designer who works on both ends of the job: the screen where a campaign gets seen, and the press where it gets made.

More than eight years across social media advertising, print production and apparel print design. Visual styles are adapted to each market and each brand's own guidelines, from Egyptian local brands to agency clients in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and France.

Experience also covers the work around the design: negotiating with clients, running a design and production department, and checking every spec before a file goes to print.
